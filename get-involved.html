Doorknocking

Sign up for a doorknocking shift.

Pick a shift that works for your schedule and we'll add you to the team. You'll be paired with an experienced volunteer, and we'll send you the meeting spot and details a couple of days before your shift.

  • No experience needed — we'll walk you through it
  • Shifts typically run 2–3 hours
  • Come solo or bring a friend

We'll follow up with your exact meeting time and location before your shift.

Host an Event

Host a meet & greet.

One of the most effective things you can do is invite a handful of your neighbours to your home for a casual conversation with Liam. No formality, no presentations — just honest conversation about North Van and what matters to the people in your community.

We take care of everything: materials, prep, and follow-up. All you need is a space and a willingness to open your door.

Get in Touch to Host
1
