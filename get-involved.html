Host an Event

Host a meet & greet.

One of the most effective things you can do is invite a handful of your neighbours to your home for a casual conversation with Liam. No formality, no presentations — just honest conversation about North Van and what matters to the people in your community.

We take care of everything: materials, prep, and follow-up. All you need is a space and a willingness to open your door.

Get in Touch to Host
1
Reach out

Contact the campaign and let us know you're interested in hosting.

2
Pick a date

We'll work with Liam's schedule to find a time that works for you and your guests.

3
Invite your neighbours

We'll help you with invitations and talking points. Aim for 5–15 people — casual is perfect.

4
Have a conversation

Liam shows up, listens, and connects. That's it. It really is that simple.
