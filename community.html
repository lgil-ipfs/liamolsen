Campaign 2025

Liam Olsen is running for North Vancouver City Council.

After months of conversations with neighbours, friends, and community members across North Vancouver, Liam has officially filed his candidacy for City Council.

"I've been listening to people across North Van for a long time," Liam said. "What I keep hearing is that people want a council that shows up, that listens, and that actually delivers. That's what I'm going to do."

The campaign has already begun — in community conversations, at local events, and through the growing group of North Van residents who are getting involved.
